Dongpai Privacy Policy

Camera images and AI composition analysis are processed locally on your phone.

Updated October 14, 2026 · Developer: ONECORNER

On-device processing

Dongpai analyzes camera frames and photos on your device to suggest compositions, track your selected subject and take photos. No account is required. Photos, Live Photo audio, recognized content and composition results are not sent to the developer's servers. The app does not use advertising tracking. The AI model is included with the app; composition analysis does not require uploading camera images or downloading a model.

Permissions

  • Camera: the viewfinder, subject selection, composition analysis and taking photos.
  • Add Photos: saving captured photos to your photo library.
  • Microphone: recording audio only when you choose to enable Live Photos.

You can manage permissions in iOS Settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

Local data and deletion

In-app photo copies and camera preferences are stored on your device. Clear in-app copies in the app's Settings. This does not delete photos already saved to the system photo library; manage those separately in the Photos app.

Uninstalling the app removes its local app data while keeping your system photo-library copies. This release does not collect new model test reports or provide test-report exports.

Email support and website access

If you contact us by email, ONECORNER receives your email address and message and uses them to respond to your request. Send only necessary information. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary private photos. You can request deletion of personal information in support emails using the address below.

This website has no added advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or online forms. Hosting and email providers may process connection information, such as IP addresses and access times, to provide and secure their services. This is separate from the app's on-device camera analysis.

Updates and contact

If our data practices change, we will update this page and its date. For privacy questions or requests about your personal information, contact us.
