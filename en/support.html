Take your first shot

  1. Open Dongpai, allow Camera access and tap the person or object you want to photograph.
  2. Wait for the advice. Move your phone or pinch to zoom until the white subject marker is close to the yellow target.
  3. Hold briefly. Green means your framing matches. Auto Capture is on by default and takes one photo per selection. Turn it off to use the shutter yourself.
  4. Retake returns to the camera. Tap a subject again for a new automatic shot.

The small yellow square shows focus. The larger yellow frame is your composition target. The guides show position and size, not the subject's exact outline. Green does not measure distance or guarantee photo quality.

Common questions

Why is analysis taking time?

AI runs on your iPhone. One tap is enough; wait for that analysis to finish. Repeated requests have a short rest period. When the phone is warm, let it cool before starting a new analysis. Existing guidance can continue unless iOS requires AI to stop; the manual shutter remains available.

What if my subject is no longer tracked?

Keep your subject visible and hold briefly. If tracking stops, tap the subject again. For a different composition, tap again after the current analysis and rest period finish.

Why can't I open the camera or save a photo?

Open iPhone Settings and check Dongpai's Camera and Photos permissions. Microphone access is used only for Live Photos you enable. If saving fails, the photo preview offers a retry.

How do I delete photos?

Clear in-app copies in Dongpai's Settings. Photos already saved in the system library are kept; delete them separately in the Photos app.

How do I report a problem?

Tell us the app version, iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing. Do not send passwords, verification codes or original private photos.
